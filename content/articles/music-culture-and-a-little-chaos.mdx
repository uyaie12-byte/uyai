*"When Shallipopi dropped Elon Musk, the streets went wild. When Davido dropped Fem, it became a movement. Even Ayra Starr calling herself a Sabi Girl had the internet shaking. That kind of chaos? That’s what I live for."*

I’ve always been obsessed with music, how it moves people, connects people, and defines eras. But until now, I never thought of writing about it or putting my thoughts down until my close friend and I argued about how Nigerian artists were becoming "sellouts", Lord knows I argued like mad and even went to my Chatgpt to vent. Turns out, I love writing (or at least typing furious paragraphs at 2 a.m lol).

I’ve spent so much time debating music and culture with friends, unpacking how artists like Asake evolved with their sounds and appearances, how trends shift on Tiktok and social media at large, and how the new things always seem to shake the old up.. I realized there’s so much to explore beyond the surface. These messy conversations are where the real fun is, not in the streaming numbers or big critic reviews but in the chaos of everyone’s opinions.

So I figured, why not put these thoughts into words? No experience, nothing at all, just a girl with her notes app, hot takes and cold coke lol...

This is me taking that bold step.

I’ll be writing about music, culture, and everything in between. No rules, no boxes, just my unfiltered (okay, maybe slightly filtered lol) and chaotic thoughts and opinions. So if you like a little chaos like me, expect takes like:

"Odumodublvck is redefining Nigerian rap. Why are we sleeping on him?"

"Rema is what full creative freedom looks like ".

"Music fans don’t actually want artists to evolve. They want nostalgia on repeat".

"Portable might just be the realest artist in Nigeria, and y’all are scared to admit it".

If you’ve ever argued about Wizkid vs Davido with your friends or wondered who decided what “Afrobeats music” should sound like, stick around.
